Sakin Privacy Policy

Sakin: Prayer Times & Adhan · Effective October 9, 2026

Sakin is a prayer companion app published by CoreTech ("we", "us"). We built Sakin to be calm, ad-free and respectful of your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in Sakin.
  • Your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. We never receive it.
  • Your prayer log, qada count, dhikr counts and settings stay on your device.
  • Payments are handled by Apple; purchases are verified through RevenueCat.

Location

Prayer times and the Qibla direction depend on where you are. If you allow location access, Sakin reads your position while you use the app and calculates prayer times and the Qibla entirely on your iPhone. To show the name of your city, and when you search for a city by name, Sakin uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Your coordinates are saved only on your device so the app works offline. You can also choose a city manually and never grant location access.

Compass

The Qibla compass uses your iPhone's motion and compass sensors. These readings are processed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Sakin saves the following locally on your iPhone: your chosen place and calculation method, notification and adhan preferences, language, the prayers you mark as prayed, your qada count, dhikr counts, and whether you have completed onboarding. The home screen widgets and Live Activity read this information from a container shared only between Sakin and its own widgets. None of it leaves your device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

Prayer time and adhan notifications are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. We do not run a notification server and do not receive any information about your notifications.

Quran listening

To list reciters and play recitations, Sakin requests content from the Quran Foundation API through a small relay server we operate on Cloudflare, and streams the audio from the Quran Foundation's audio servers. These requests contain only what is technically needed to deliver the content (such as your IP address and the surah and reciter you chose). They contain no account, name or location, and we do not keep logs of them. Cloudflare's and the Quran Foundation's own privacy policies apply to their services.

Purchases, subscriptions and tips

Sakin Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store, and you can leave an optional tip. Apple processes all payments; we never see your payment details. To check whether Pro is active, Sakin uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous identifier generated by the app together with your App Store purchase and transaction information, the app version and basic device information (such as the iOS version and region). We do not link this identifier to your name or Apple ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Children

Sakin is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time and choose a city manually instead. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. If you would like the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat to be deleted, contact us and we will process the request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sakin handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
