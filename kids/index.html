How does the map work?

Each stop is a short surah or dua. Listen, repeat and then play the quiz. When your child passes the quiz, the next stop opens. The first three stops are free.

How do I unlock all stops?

Tap a gift stop, answer the grown-up question, and buy the full version once. It is not a subscription, and Family Sharing lets all your children's devices use it.

I bought the full version but it is locked on a new device

Open the full version screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Can I add more than one child?

Yes. Open the parent area (the lock icon, behind the grown-up question) and add a profile for each child. Each child has their own stars and progress.

Does it need the internet?

No. All recitations are inside the app, so it works on a plane, in the car, anywhere.
