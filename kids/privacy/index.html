Sakin Kids Privacy Policy

Sakin Kids: Surahs & Duas · Updated November 23, 2026

Sakin Kids helps children learn short surahs and duas with listening, repetition and quizzes. It is published by CoreTech ("we", "us"), the makers of Sakin. We built it for children, so it collects nothing.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information about children or parents.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no chat.
  • The app works fully offline. All recitations and texts are inside the app; it does not connect to our servers.
  • The only purchase is a one-time full version, handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), behind a parental gate.

Information stored on the device

To remember progress, the app saves on the device only: the child profile names and avatars you create, which stops are completed, stars, levels, streaks, badges, the reward goal a parent sets, and app settings (language, reading aid). This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The first three stops are free. The full version is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Payment is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we do not receive your payment details. Before the purchase screen opens, a parental gate (a simple math question) makes sure an adult is present.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. The app is designed to comply with Apple's Kids Category rules and with children's privacy laws such as COPPA and the GDPR.

Recitation and texts

The Quran recitation is by Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary (teaching recitation). Arabic text, word timings and translations were prepared in advance and are bundled with the app, so no request is made while the app is used.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
